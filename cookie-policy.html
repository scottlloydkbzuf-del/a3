Legal Compliance

Digital Navigation & Session Cookie Management

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Institutional Publication • 181 Mercer Street

1. Institutional Scope and Regulatory Framework

At Shallot Aroma, operating our gastronomic atelier from 181 Mercer Street, safeguarding the confidentiality and integrity of all patron records is our highest corporate commitment. We maintain rigorous administrative safeguards to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All electronic communications, private dining reservations, and transactional records are encrypted using modern industrial security standards. We do not sell or lease your proprietary identity to outside advertising syndicates under any circumstance.

2. Transparent Patron Information Protocols

When reserving dining seatings or communicating with our culinary concierge, we collect necessary contact information including your full name, email address, dietary restrictions, and direct telephone number. This information allows our staff to coordinate tasting flights, accommodate allergies, and maintain transparent service documentation. Furthermore, our web servers automatically log standard network telemetry including internet protocol addresses, browser types, and timestamped page visits to ensure optimal platform performance and digital security.

3. Technical Cookies and Session Architecture

Our digital infrastructure employs strictly essential session cookies to facilitate rapid navigation, remember user accessibility preferences, and ensure seamless reservation submission. These technical cookies do not harvest external tracking records or monitor your personal browsing habits beyond our primary domain. Patrons may adjust their private browser preferences to disable non-essential cookie storage at any time without compromising essential dining reservation functions on this website.

4. Secure Record Retention and Automated Purge

We store patron records exclusively for durations necessary to fulfill explicit dining purposes, complete culinary reservations, and satisfy mandatory municipal food service accounting rules. Once our service relationship concludes and statutory storage periods lapse, your identifiable records are permanently erased from our secure cloud infrastructure. You maintain full statutory rights to request an audited extract of all personal data held in our systems at any moment.

5. Operational Amendments and Statutory Revisions

We reserve the unilateral operational authority to update this institutional policy whenever statutory frameworks change or our internal technical reservation systems undergo modernization. Any material modifications will be promptly published on this dedicated URL with an updated legal revision date. Continued engagement with our digital services following published revisions constitutes formal acceptance of our modernized administrative protocols and client protection measures.

6. Institutional Compliance Contact Desk

If you require legal clarification regarding our privacy protocols, wish to exercise statutory data rights, or need assistance regarding your records, please contact our privacy compliance desk directly. You may reach us by writing to our headquarters at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by placing a telephone call to our verified office line at +1-888-777-5845 during standard business hours.
