Culinary Inquiries & Guidance

Details regarding dietary accommodations, tasting durations, and allium provenance.

Can dining accommodations be made for allium sensitivities?

Because our core philosophy centers upon the historical gastronomy of alliums, our tasting flights celebrate shallots, leeks, and chives in every course. While we cater to gluten, dairy, and seafood restrictions with advance notice, an allium-free menu is contrary to the culinary mandate of Shallot Aroma.

What is the typical duration of the Grand Tasting Flight?

Our eight-course tasting flight is paced unhurriedly over two and a half to three hours. We encourage guests to relax, converse, and fully immerse themselves in the aromatic evolution of each plate.

How are private events booked at 181 Mercer Street?

Private dining salon reservations for groups of twelve to twenty-four can be arranged by calling our concierge desk at +1-888-777-5845. Our executive chef designs personalized commemorative menus for each private gathering.

Where do you source your French grey shallots?

We partner directly with family-run farms in the coastal sandy soils of Brittany and the Val de Loire. Each crop is harvested by hand at peak maturity and air-cured in traditional timber barns.
