Our Origin Story

The Passion Behind the Allium Atelier

Born from a lifelong obsession with the cornerstone baseal building blocks of classical cuisine, Shallot Aroma was established to liberate the humble shallot from the background and place it where it belongs: in the spotlight.

Rooted at 181 Mercer Street

Our flagship kitchen and dining salon occupies a historic cast-iron space in Manhattan. Here, our brigade combines classical French saucier techniques with contemporary culinary science.

We source exclusively from heirloom seed growers who refuse hybrid commercial varieties. The French *Échalote Grise* (grey shallot) remains our crown jewel—prized for its subtle pungency and unmatched sweetness upon caramelization.
